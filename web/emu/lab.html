Modulation matrix

AT is aftertouch, MODW the mod wheel, VEL each note's velocity. Try AT → CUT on a reed, AT → AMP on a pipe.

The Quinte, section by section

La Quinte Estampie Real, as the ESTAMPIE scene plays it: the rauschpfeife on the tune, the lute, the gurdy's drones and the tabor. Play each section, tick the ones to keep, then hear them as a song. Playing here loads the ESTAMPIE scene's sounds into the four parts; the TROTTO's Play puts the TROTTO back as it starts.

Tempo

The sound is the firmware itself (its engines, sequencer, reverb and tape) compiled for the browser, so what you hear is what the FM-1 plays. Copy a sound you like as a preset and send it over: that line goes straight into the firmware. Nothing here changes your FM-1.

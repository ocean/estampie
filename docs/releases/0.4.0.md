ESTAMPIE 0.4.0: a whole estampie to play, and five new instruments.

**Install it** from the browser at https://fm-1.drewr.dev/estampie/webapp/installer/ (Chrome or Edge), or install the `.fwsc` with FM1Kit or FM-SWITCH. **Try it first** in your browser at https://fm-1.drewr.dev/estampie/webapp/emu/.

### What's new

- **The ESTAMPIE scene:** *La Quinte Estampie Real*, a French royal dance from about 1300, played on a rauschpfeife, a lute, a hurdy-gurdy's drones and the tabor. Press PLAY and it plays the first two sections, each twice, then a tabor solo, then both sections again at 148 BPM: about two minutes, then round again. The header shows which section is playing. Turn the tempo and the rest of the dance follows you. Change a note, load a pattern or record, and PLAY plays the tracks as they are again.
- **Load a scene any time:** hold HOME for the menu, turn SYSTEM › SCENE to a scene and press OCT+. It asks first, because it replaces all four tracks.
- **Scenes set the tuning:** the ESTAMPIE scene plays in Pythagorean tuning (TEMP PYTH, pure fifths from C), so its drones ring. Every other scene starts in equal temperament, and loading a sound never changes a track's tuning.
- **Five new instruments**, each chosen by ear in the Sound Lab:
  - RAUSCHPFEIFE (TRIO), a capped shawm, rounder and softer than SHAWM;
  - CRUMHORN (TRIO), buzzy and nasal;
  - PSALTERY (PHYS), plucked, with strings an octave up ringing along;
  - BRAY HARP (PHYS), a gothic harp with its bray pins' buzz;
  - PORTATIVE (WHEEL), a small organ with a breathy chiff.
- **REC held at power-on** for a second goes straight to the FM-1's download mode, as m-bootloader does, for recovery with FM1Rescue or M-Flasher.
- **The guide** has the new scene, an estampie band recipe and the new instruments in the others.
- **Built from source, and signed,** like every release since 0.3.1: check a file with `gh attestation verify estampie-0.4.0.fwsc --repo ocean/estampie --signer-workflow zvenson/fm1-build/.github/workflows/build.yml`, or rebuild it and compare with `tools/verify_release.sh 0.4.0`.

ESTAMPIE 0.3.0, the reader release: the FM-1's screen can now be read aloud, so you can play and edit ESTAMPIE without seeing its screen.

**Install it** from the browser at https://fm-1.drewr.dev/estampie/webapp/installer/ (Chrome or Edge), or install the `.fwsc` with FM1Kit or FM-SWITCH. **Try it first** in your browser at https://fm-1.drewr.dev/estampie/webapp/emu/.

### What's new

- **A screen reader for the FM-1 (new):** ESTAMPIE tells a screen reader on your computer what its screen shows, using Charles Vestal's [fm1-reader](https://github.com/charlesvestal/fm1-reader).
  - Plug the FM-1 in, open https://charlesvestal.github.io/fm1-reader/ in Chrome or Edge, and press **Connect**. It reads with its own voice, or hands the words to your own screen reader (VoiceOver, NVDA, JAWS, TalkBack).
  - **When it connects, or when you ask:** the track and its sound, the page and its four knobs, the steps that are on, and whether it's playing.
  - **As you play:** each new page with its knobs, a knob's value once you stop turning it, the menu's rows with their place ("3 of 8"), the sound the PRESETS knob picks, and messages. Warnings are read in full, questions come with the buttons that answer them, and each letter is read as you name a sound or a project.
  - **ESTAMPIE's own words:** "ATK 12 ms" is read as "attack 12 milliseconds" and "DLVL" as "drone level". There are 276 words in all, checked against fm1-reader's rules with no errors. The words come from this site, so they cost the firmware no flash.
  - **No change for other software:** the FM-1 says nothing until a reader asks, so a DAW on the same USB port sees nothing new.
- **The browser version reads its screen too:** under its panel, choose **Your screen reader** or **This page's voice**, then use **Read the whole screen** and **Say it again**.
- **A new preview picture** for links to the repository and the site.

### Good to know

- This is new and tested in the simulator and the browser version; please tell us how it goes on a real FM-1.
- Firmware size: the screen reader adds about 6 KB, with about 34 KB still free.
- The guide's chapter 20 has a new section, "A screen reader": https://fm-1.drewr.dev/estampie/guide/
